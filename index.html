Moving across state lines? Read the FMCSA's Your Rights and Responsibilities When You Move, which we publish on our site.

When should I call to book my move?

If you need to move today, we can probably do that. If you'd like a written, in-home estimate, call about 7 days to 2 weeks before your move. Weekends, the end of the month and holidays are often booked weeks or months in advance, so the more notice the better.

How do you charge?

Local moves are typically billed at an hourly rate, and our time doesn't start until we arrive on the job and ends when your move is complete. No hidden charges. Long-distance and interstate moves are billed from an estimate, and we provide binding estimates, so your price won't change unless you change destinations or add items.

Is insurance included?

Yes. Basic insurance coverage is free for every customer, and pad wrapping and shrink wrap are included at no extra cost. We'll help you choose the right level of protection, and some high-value items or collections may need additional valuation coverage.

Are you a moving company or a broker?

We're a real mover. Our own crews and our own fleet of clean, fully equipped trucks do your move. The FMCSA lists AM PM Movers as an active carrier authorized for household goods (USDOT 897422, MC-390135).

What questions will you ask me?

Your pickup and destination addresses, how many bedrooms you have, and any special items such as a piano, pool table, tanning bed, gun safe or hot tub. Also tell us about stairs or limited truck access at either end.

What can’t go on the truck?

Drugs, alcohol, weapons, ammunition, fuels and flammables. Keep jewelry and other high-value items with you. Not sure about something? Just call and ask.

Can I leave clothes in my drawers?

Yes. Just don't leave anything solid in them that could damage the piece.

How much should I tip?

Tipping isn't required, but it's appreciated. A good rule of thumb is about $5 per hour, per mover.

How can I pay?

Most major credit and debit cards, cashier's checks, money orders, traveler's checks and U.S. currency. Personal checks must be drawn on a local bank and include your driver's license number.
